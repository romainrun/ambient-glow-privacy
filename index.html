Privacy Policy – Ambient Glow

Last updated: December 12, 2025

Ambient Glow (“the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Ambient Glow does not require an account and does not ask you to provide personal information (such as your name, email, or address).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ambient Glow uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner ads (for example on the home screen). AdMob may collect and process certain device information such as advertising identifiers and approximate location (depending on your device settings), in order to provide, measure, and improve ads.

How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Pro Version (Ad-free)

If you purchase the Pro version, ads will be disabled in the app.

4. Children’s Privacy

Ambient Glow is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
