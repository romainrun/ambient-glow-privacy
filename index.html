Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Ambient Glow ("the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user and device data.

1. Data Collection

Ambient Glow does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

The app does not require user accounts, login, or personal data input.

2. Advertising

Ambient Glow uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner advertisements on the home screen.

AdMob may collect limited, non-personal data (such as device information or advertising identifiers) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Users who purchase the Pro version will not see ads.

For more information, please review Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Children’s Privacy

Ambient Glow is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

The app does not contain content intended to target children specifically.

4. Data Security

Since Ambient Glow does not collect personal data, no personal information is stored or processed by the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
