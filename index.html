Privacy Policy – Ambient Glow

Last updated: December 12, 2025

Ambient Glow (“the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Ambient Glow does not require user accounts and does not ask for personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ambient Glow displays non-intrusive banner advertisements using Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal data such as device information, advertising identifiers, and approximate location (depending on your device settings) to provide, personalize, and measure ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Pro Version (Ad-Free)

Ambient Glow offers a Pro version as a one-time purchase. When Pro is activated, all advertisements are removed from the app.

4. Children’s Privacy

Ambient Glow is intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

5. Data Security

Ambient Glow does not store personal information on its own servers. Any data processed by advertising partners is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
